# Industry Layouts

Structured layout guide for 15 industries. The designer-agent uses this as a baseline and customizes based on live competitor/award research. The architect-agent references this for the recommended page set.

Each entry defines: recommended pages, homepage section order, hero style, navigation pattern, conversion strategy, must-have elements, and common mistakes to avoid.

---

## Manufacturing / Industrial

- **Pages:** Home, About, Products/Capabilities, Industries Served, Quality/Certifications, Contact, Request Quote
- **Homepage sections:** Hero → Client Logos → Capabilities Overview → Featured Products → Stats/Counters → Why Choose Us → Certifications/Quality → CTA → Contact/Quote Form → Footer
- **Hero:** Clear product/facility image, headline states what they manufacture, "Request a Quote" primary CTA
- **Navigation:** Products by category/application, "Industries Served" as top-level nav, persistent "Get a Quote" CTA in header
- **Conversion:** Quote forms on every product page, downloadable spec sheets/catalogs, file upload in RFQ
- **Must-have:** ISO certifications displayed, real facility photos, technical specifications, case studies (Problem→Solution→Result)
- **Avoid:** Stock factory photos, vague capabilities, hiding quote form

## B2B SaaS / Software

- **Pages:** Home, Features, Pricing, About, Blog, Contact, Login/Signup, Docs/API
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (value prop + CTA + product screenshot) → Social Proof → Features Grid → How It Works → Testimonials/Case Studies → Pricing Preview → Integrations → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Product screenshot or demo, outcome-focused headline, dual CTA ("Start Free Trial" + "Book a Demo")
- **Navigation:** Features, Pricing, Docs/API as top-level nav, Login + CTA in header
- **Conversion:** Free trial/demo on every page, pricing transparency, competitor comparison table
- **Must-have:** Real product screenshots, integration ecosystem, security badges, customer metrics
- **Avoid:** Feature lists without outcomes, hiding pricing, signup-wall before seeing anything

## Restaurant / Hospitality

- **Pages:** Home, Menu, About, Events, Reservations, Gallery, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (food/ambiance photo) → Specials/Featured Items → About Snippet → Menu Preview → Testimonials/Reviews → Location/Hours → Reservation CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** High-quality food or ambiance photo, name + tagline, "Reserve a Table" or "Order Online" CTA
- **Navigation:** Menu, Reservations, Location/Hours prominent, "Order Online" CTA if applicable
- **Conversion:** Online reservation integration, visible phone number, menu accessible without download
- **Must-have:** Menu (not just PDF), hours and location, real food photos, reviews integration
- **Avoid:** PDF-only menus, outdated specials, stock food photos, hard-to-find hours

## Healthcare / Medical

- **Pages:** Home, Services, About/Providers, Patient Resources, Insurance/Billing, Contact, Appointments
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (welcoming facility/staff image) → Services Overview → Meet the Providers → Insurance Accepted → Patient Testimonials → Location/Hours → Appointment CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Professional, welcoming photo (staff or facility), clear specialty statement, "Book Appointment" CTA
- **Navigation:** Services, Providers, Patient Resources, Insurance as top-level nav, appointment CTA persistent
- **Conversion:** Online appointment booking, visible phone number, patient portal link
- **Must-have:** Provider credentials and photos, accepted insurance list, HIPAA compliance notice, accessibility compliance
- **Avoid:** Stock medical photos, missing credentials, complex medical jargon for patient-facing content

## E-commerce / Retail

- **Pages:** Home, Shop (category pages), Product Pages, About, Contact, Cart, Checkout, Blog (optional)
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (featured product/sale) → Categories → Featured Products → Social Proof/Reviews → Value Props (shipping, returns) → Newsletter Signup → Footer
- **Hero:** Featured product or seasonal promotion, clear value proposition, "Shop Now" CTA
- **Navigation:** Product categories, search bar, cart icon with count, account/login
- **Conversion:** Add-to-cart on every product, transparent shipping costs, easy returns policy, abandoned cart recovery
- **Must-have:** Product reviews, high-quality product photos (multiple angles), size guides, filtering/sorting
- **Avoid:** Low-quality product photos, hidden shipping costs, complex checkout, no reviews

## Legal / Accounting

- **Pages:** Home, Practice Areas/Services, Attorneys/Team, Results/Case Studies, Blog, Contact, Free Consultation
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (professional team/office photo) → Practice Areas → Featured Results → Attorney Profiles → Testimonials → Awards/Recognition → CTA → Contact Form → Footer
- **Hero:** Professional photo, specialty + location, "Free Consultation" CTA
- **Navigation:** Practice Areas, Our Team, Results as top-level nav, consultation CTA persistent
- **Conversion:** Free consultation forms, visible phone number, chat/callback option
- **Must-have:** Attorney credentials and bar associations, case results (anonymized), testimonials, practice area detail
- **Avoid:** Generic stock photos, vague service descriptions, hiding contact information

## Real Estate

- **Pages:** Home, Listings, Services (Buy/Sell/Rent), About/Agent Profile, Neighborhoods/Areas, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (featured property or search bar) → Featured Listings → Services → Neighborhood Guides → Testimonials → Market Stats → Agent Profile → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Property search bar or featured listing, location-focused headline, "Search Homes" or "Schedule Showing" CTA
- **Navigation:** Buy, Sell, Rent, Neighborhoods as top-level nav, agent contact persistent
- **Conversion:** Property search, listing inquiry forms, market report downloads, home valuation tool
- **Must-have:** IDX/MLS integration reference, neighborhood expertise, sold properties, professional headshot
- **Avoid:** Low-quality listing photos, no neighborhood context, missing licensing info

## Education / Training

- **Pages:** Home, Programs/Courses, About, Faculty/Instructors, Admissions/Enrollment, Student Resources, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (campus/students photo) → Programs Overview → Why Choose Us → Student Success Stories → Faculty Highlights → Admissions CTA → Events → Footer
- **Hero:** Campus or student photo, clear value proposition, "Apply Now" or "Explore Programs" CTA
- **Navigation:** Programs, Admissions, Student Resources as top-level nav, "Apply" CTA persistent
- **Conversion:** Application/enrollment forms, program comparison, tuition transparency, campus visit scheduling
- **Must-have:** Accreditation badges, student outcomes/statistics, faculty credentials, clear tuition information
- **Avoid:** Stock classroom photos, vague outcomes, hidden costs, complex application process

## Creative Agency / Design

- **Pages:** Home, Work/Portfolio, Services, About, Process, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (bold statement or featured project) → Selected Work → Services → Process → Client Logos → Testimonials → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Bold creative statement or hero project, minimal text, "See Our Work" or "Start a Project" CTA
- **Navigation:** Minimal, clean — Work, Services, About, Contact. CTA in header
- **Conversion:** Project inquiry forms, portfolio case studies with results, process transparency
- **Must-have:** Real project work (not mockups), case studies with metrics, team photos, design process
- **Avoid:** Template-looking design, stock imagery, vague service descriptions, no case study results

## Professional Services

- **Pages:** Home, Services, About/Team, Case Studies, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (team/office photo) → Services Overview → Case Studies → Why Choose Us → Team → Testimonials → CTA → Contact → Footer
- **Hero:** Professional team photo, clear service statement, "Get a Consultation" CTA
- **Navigation:** Services, About, Case Studies, Blog, Contact. CTA in header
- **Conversion:** Consultation forms, downloadable resources (guides, whitepapers), case study detail pages
- **Must-have:** Team credentials, industry-specific case studies, clear methodology, client logos
- **Avoid:** Generic descriptions, no team visibility, missing credentials, vague process

## Automotive

- **Pages:** Home, Inventory/Vehicles, Services, About, Financing, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (featured vehicle or dealership) → Inventory Highlights → Services → Financing Options → Customer Reviews → Location/Hours → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Featured vehicle or dealership photo, "Browse Inventory" or "Schedule Service" CTA
- **Navigation:** Inventory (by type/make), Services, Financing, About. Search bar for inventory
- **Conversion:** Inventory search/filter, financing calculator, service appointment booking, trade-in valuation
- **Must-have:** High-quality vehicle photos, transparent pricing, service menu, financing options
- **Avoid:** Low-quality photos, hidden pricing, complex navigation, no online scheduling

## Finance / Insurance

- **Pages:** Home, Services, About/Team, Resources/Blog, Tools/Calculators, Contact, Client Portal
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (professional photo) → Services → Why Choose Us → Team → Tools/Calculators → Testimonials → Trust Badges → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Professional, trustworthy imagery, clear value proposition, "Schedule Consultation" CTA
- **Navigation:** Services, About, Resources, Tools. Client portal link + CTA in header
- **Conversion:** Consultation scheduling, financial calculators, downloadable guides, client portal access
- **Must-have:** Credentials and licenses, compliance disclosures, security certifications, calculator tools
- **Avoid:** Stock money photos, missing compliance info, vague service descriptions, no trust indicators

## Construction / Trades

- **Pages:** Home, Services, Projects/Portfolio, About, Testimonials, Blog, Contact, Request Estimate
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (project photo) → Services → Featured Projects → Why Choose Us → Stats (years, projects completed) → Testimonials → Service Area → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Completed project photo, service + location, "Get a Free Estimate" CTA
- **Navigation:** Services, Projects, About, Contact. "Get Estimate" CTA persistent
- **Conversion:** Estimate request forms, project galleries with before/after, visible phone number
- **Must-have:** Licensing and insurance displayed, project galleries, before/after photos, service area map
- **Avoid:** Stock construction photos, missing credentials, no project examples, hidden service area

## Fitness / Wellness

- **Pages:** Home, Programs/Classes, About/Trainers, Schedule, Pricing/Membership, Blog, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (action shot or facility) → Programs → Trainer Profiles → Class Schedule → Testimonials/Transformations → Pricing → Location/Hours → CTA → Footer
- **Hero:** Action shot or facility photo, motivational headline, "Start Your Journey" or "Book a Class" CTA
- **Navigation:** Programs, Schedule, Pricing, Trainers. "Join Now" CTA persistent
- **Conversion:** Class booking, free trial offer, membership signup, transformation galleries
- **Must-have:** Trainer certifications, class schedule, real client transformations, facility photos
- **Avoid:** Intimidating imagery, hidden pricing, no schedule, stock fitness photos

## Nonprofit

- **Pages:** Home, About/Mission, Programs, Impact/Results, Get Involved, Donate, Blog/News, Contact
- **Homepage sections:** Hero (mission-driven imagery) → Mission Statement → Programs Overview → Impact Stats → Stories/Testimonials → Get Involved → Donate CTA → Newsletter → Footer
- **Hero:** Mission-driven photography, emotional headline, "Donate" + "Get Involved" dual CTA
- **Navigation:** About, Programs, Impact, Get Involved, Donate. "Donate" CTA persistent and prominent
- **Conversion:** Donation flow (one-time + recurring), volunteer signup, newsletter, event registration
- **Must-have:** Impact metrics, financial transparency, beneficiary stories, clear mission statement
- **Avoid:** Guilt-based messaging, hidden financials, complex donation process, no impact evidence

## General / Other (Fallback)

When the discovery-agent can't match one of the 15 defined industries, use the **Professional Services** layout as a generic baseline. It works for most businesses because services + about + testimonials + contact is universally applicable.

- Default animation tier: **Tier 2 (Moderate)**
- Default layout: Professional Services (above)
- The discovery-agent documents:
  ```
  Industry: Other
  Sub-category: [specific business type]
  Closest match: [nearest industry from the 15]
  Reasoning: [why this match]
  ```
